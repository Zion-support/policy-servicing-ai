## What it does
- **Endorsement automation** — draft, validate and issue mid-term changes with full audit trail.
- **COI & evidence requests** — auto-generate certificates and evidence of insurance in seconds.
- **Billing & payment copilot** — answer billing inquiries, predict delinquency, draft outreach.
- **Renewal readiness** — pre-assemble renewal files; hand off to [Broker Renewal Copilot](https://github.com/Zion-support/broker-renewal-copilot).
